# What Employer Brand Auditor does not do

Employer Brand Auditor does not currently:

- **Log in to social networks or get around their protections.** Closed platforms are covered only through screenshots you add.
- **Treat employee reviews as facts.** Reviews appear as a separate perception layer, as "N of M reviews mention…", and never change the score.
- **Use what it might "know" about a company.** When you name a company, it collects the company's own public candidate-facing pages and audits only their text. It does not use memory, news, review sites, salary aggregators or employees' personal profiles.
- **Verify employer claims against external sources automatically.** Facts in your material are treated as not independently checked unless you supply an independent source.
- **Compare you with other employers or the market.** The score is measured against the method, not against competitors, and the auditor has no market data.
- **Benchmark compensation or benefits** against a market, and does not say whether pay is competitive.
- **Provide legal advice** or check job ads for legal compliance. Where wording might matter legally, it suggests a specialist review.
- **Replace candidate research.** It judges how the material reads to a candidate; it does not measure what real candidates think.
- **Replace employee research.** It cannot tell you what employees actually experience.
- **Measure actual employee sentiment** or engagement.
- **Infer internal practices that are not evidenced.** If the material does not show something, the audit says so and does not conclude that it is missing.
- **Guarantee the factual correctness of rewritten copy without review.** Rewrites use only facts from your material, but they are drafts. Review them before publishing.

Also outside its scope:
- comparisons with competitors you have not supplied material for;
- general HR, recruitment-process or legal consulting;
- remembering previous audits between sessions.
