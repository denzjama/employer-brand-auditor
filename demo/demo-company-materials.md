## Material 3 — Job advertisement

**Lift Service Engineer — Westmere North**

**Salary:** £38,000–£44,000 plus on-call allowance
**Hours:** 40 hours a week, Monday to Friday, plus on-call one week in four
**Location:** Field-based, with a company van

**About the role**
You will service, test and repair passenger and goods lifts across our northern patch, which includes two hospitals and more than 60 residential blocks. You will work alone on most routine visits and in pairs for major repairs.

**About you**
- NVQ Level 3 in lift engineering or equivalent
- Full UK driving licence
- Able to work in a fast-paced environment
- A team player with a can-do attitude

**What we offer**
- Flexible working
- 25 days' holiday plus bank holidays
- Pension and health cash plan
- Training and development opportunities

To apply, send your CV to careers@quellmoor.example. We aim to reply within 10 working days.

## Material 5 — Employee story

**"The first winter on call nearly put me off." — Ilse Varga, Lift Service Engineer**

"I joined Quellmoor as an apprentice in 2019, straight from college. My mentor, Joe, had been with the company for 22 years, and for the first year I went everywhere with him.

The first winter on call was hard. I remember a 2am call to Westmere General: a lift was stuck between floors with a patient trolley inside. Joe talked me through it on the phone, but I was the one in the motor room.

I qualified in 2023. This year I started mentoring two apprentices myself. I still don't love being on call in January, but I know why it matters."
