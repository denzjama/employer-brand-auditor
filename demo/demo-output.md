# Quellmoor Lift & Access: employer brand audit

**Score: 63/100.** That falls in the 50–69 band, which means the materials work but read as generic or unsupported in important places. All 34 criteria could be assessed. The score is reasonably reliable because four types of material were supplied: an employer proposition, a careers page, job ads and an employee story.

**Verdict:** You have specific proof of essential, demanding work, but it sits in the brand guide, one job ad and one employee story. The careers page and the coordinator ad make their case with phrases any comparable employer could use.

## Key findings
- **Your most persuasive material isn't on the careers page.** That material is the hospital response figures, the apprentice pairing and Ilse Varga's story, and only you can make that case. The careers page offers four stock bullets instead, such as "A supportive, close-knit team".
- **The two job ads differ a lot.** The engineer ad gives what a candidate needs to decide: pay range, hours, how often on-call comes round, the patch and the van. The coordinator ad gives no salary and opens with a stock line.
- **Two promises don't fit the roles they're attached to.**
  - "Flexible working" appears in the engineer ad, next to a fixed 40-hour Monday-to-Friday week with on-call one week in four.
  - The guide's development proof covers apprentices only, but the engineer ad already requires NVQ Level 3.
- **On-call is the honest thread running through the set.** The ad states one week in four, and Ilse says her first winter on call nearly put her off. Combined with the hospital work, this is the most ownable idea in the material. At the moment, "Like a family" and "Flexible working" sit where it could be.

## What to do first
1. **Rebuild the "Why join us?" section on the careers page** using the figures, apprentice pairing and Ilse's story you already have. No new information is needed.
2. **Fix the coordinator ad.** Add the salary, the shift pattern and how long training lasts, and replace the stock opening line.
3. **Spell out flexible working and post-qualification development for engineers,** or remove both promises.
