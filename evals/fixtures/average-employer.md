## Document 3 — Job advertisement

**Warehouse Team Leader — Wigmoor Distribution Centre**

**Pay:** £15.20 per hour, plus 10% premium for night shifts
**Shift pattern:** 4 on / 4 off, 06:00–18:00 or 18:00–06:00
**Contract:** Permanent, full time

**The role**
You'll lead a team of 12–15 operatives picking, packing and loading orders for our hospital and pharmacy customers. You'll make sure the team hits its pick-rate and accuracy targets, keep the floor safe, and hand over clearly to the next shift.

**What we're looking for**
- Experience working in a warehouse environment
- A passion for excellence
- Strong communication skills
- Previous supervisory experience is helpful but not essential — we'll train you through the Hallbrook Academy

**Why Hallbrook?**
We're a dynamic, growing business with great opportunities for people who want to get on.

**How to apply**
Click "Apply" — it takes about 5 minutes and you don't need a cover letter.
